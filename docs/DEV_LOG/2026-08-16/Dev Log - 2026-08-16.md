# Narrative Log - 2026-08-16

This was a day of taking the shine off several misleading surfaces. The Connections control plane had enough structure to be useful, but the useful correction was making it stop advertising channels that were not actually wired. Setup truth now follows the real boundary instead of the optimism of a catalog entry.

The Tester work followed the same pattern. An OOM characterization is valuable because it tells us what failed and under what conditions, but it is not a capacity fix or a healthy startup. The proof handoff was corrected so the failure remains legible rather than becoming an accidental claim of remediation.

The browser context work gave another implicit assumption a contract. Episodic context and recall are not automatically durable memory, and visible context is not automatically authority. ADR-070 creates a place to reason about what is observed, what can be persisted, and what must be reconstructed without silently turning browser state into ownership.

The common change underneath all three workstreams is refusal to advertise beyond evidence. Connections, tester capacity, and browser recall now expose narrower truths. That makes the next implementation step more constrained, but also more testable.

The next phase is to prove the owning boundaries one at a time: authenticated connector setup, a repaired or explicitly quarantined tester runtime, and a browser-context path whose persistence and authority are both visible.
