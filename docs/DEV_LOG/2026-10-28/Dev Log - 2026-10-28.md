## Narrative Log

# Dev Log - 2026-10-28

The shape of this day was consolidation, and what was being consolidated was a migration lineage that had quietly become three. Saved Widget, memory consent, and recipient-local read state were all in flight at once, each carrying its own Alembic head. Any one of those branches on its own was fine. Together they meant the mainline could not be migrated with confidence, because "head" had stopped being a single answer. The work through the day made it single again: merge the consent and Saved Widget heads, integrate the read-state branch onto the reconciled tree, and then verify the reconciled head before promoting it rather than after.

That ordering is a small decision with large consequences. It is easy to merge three heads and then discover the combination needs repair. Doing it the other way round means the thing being promoted is a thing that has already been checked. The receipts recorded here are bounded in the right way: a backend suite result on that tree, a frontend evidence transfer onto it, a rebase-compatibility receipt, and a correction to how `synchronous_commit` was characterised, naming the two node ids that were still outstanding instead of leaving them implicit. Each is code and test evidence at a specific tree. None of it is live supported-Compose qualification, and the release gate stayed exactly where it was.

Alongside that, the release-posture checks were tightened three times in a single day, which says something about the failure mode being defended against. Not that a claim was wrong, but that a contradictory claim could be phrased in a way the checker still accepted. Loosely matched phrasing is precisely how "qualified" and "ready" drift into one another. The posture work now rejects those, and the operator-actor and human-approval boundary was written down as a merged ADR with collision-safe campaign approval provenance beside it: governance that says who may act and on whose approval, without pretending that a credential is an identity.

The smaller work applied the same discipline to the harness itself. Isolating a Postgres orphan check inside a SQLite fixture. Preserving pytest logging across Alembic migrations so a failing migration could still speak for itself. Correcting a bounded task-event fixture. Refreshing the current-state override and the knowledge-graph hashes so the knowledge graph stopped describing a tree that no longer existed. Uncelebrated, and worth more than the celebrated work.

What did not change deserves equal care. The feature-ref work — Saved Widget revisions and eligibility, generative-UI containment, the coding-worker claim schema, the CI stall diagnostics — was rebased onto the unified head and remained unintegrated. Nothing here made a Saved Widget promotable, made containment cross-browser, or moved the supported path out of HOLD.

The day ended with a repository whose history had one migration head and whose claims had been re-anchored to that head. The next honest step is not another merge. It is a fresh supported-Compose qualification against the tip that now exists.
