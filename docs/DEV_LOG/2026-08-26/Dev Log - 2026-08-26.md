# Narrative Log - 2026-08-26

Today was a qualification day, and the useful work was in keeping the qualifications separate. The repository moved through tester recovery, Chroma diagnosis, Guardian/Pi invocation, Watchdog setup, image retention, and Campaign Engine validation, but none of those boundaries were allowed to borrow certainty from the others.

The tester path produced the clearest positive result: one proof-only account completed an ordinary local Gemma turn through the worker and queue path, with API, Postgres, Redis-stream, and assistant-message readback agreeing. That is a real runtime observation. It is still a tester-stack observation, not proof that the current supported Compose tip is healthy or that retrieval is closed.

Chroma provided the counterexample. The historical derived index was preserved and retired, and the old panic did not recur in the guarded recovery attempt. The fresh state then failed at another startup boundary. The diagnosis classified the failure against an empty-store control and stopped there. No retry, restore, or implementation change was smuggled in under the label of recovery.

The Guardian/Pi lane moved forward more cleanly. The repaired wrapper matched the frozen provider, model, harness, and permission identity for one bounded invocation against a read-only disposable target, with no retry or fallback. That is enough to requalify the named seam. It is not enough to promote general provider support or unattended Campaign Engine execution.

The Campaign Engine work translated that same discipline into validation rules. A live attempt must actually use a live executor binding, preserve provider/model identity, and keep granted permissions within the requested set. The supervised-usability closure then froze the next dependency order without adding scheduling, background execution, automatic repair, or autonomous repository mutation.

The remaining work was operationally honest. Watchdog qualification stopped before review execution when its explicit model policy was missing or unavailable. Image-retention evidence showed that a qualified immutable image was gone and the mutable tag had moved, while leaving the deletion mechanism unknown. The disposable archive experiment stopped before Docker mutation because the intended authority was unavailable.

The deeper change is that the system now carries the reason a proof stopped alongside the proof itself. A passing Pi invocation, a tester chat turn, a worker queue loop, and a schema validator each say something specific. They no longer have to pretend to say everything. The next step is to apply that same precision to the current-main supported Compose closure.
