# Dev Log - 2026-11-10

## Summary

A day of recovery proof, and the day the recovery proof narrowed itself. The work began with a straightforward question — can the Private Preview stack be frozen, dumped, and restored safely enough that OpenAI conversations can be materialized into ordinary Projects? — and ended with a much smaller, much more defensible claim: a verified PostgreSQL-only logical checkpoint, with a SHA-256 on the archive and matching ownership and relationship digests on the restore, while the full-stack backup remained UNVERIFIED and the release posture remained HOLD.

That narrowing is the day's real character. Three times in twelve hours the proof refused to proceed — first because the helper's freeze used a reduced Compose configuration that could restart services against different images, then because the serving identity was not coherent enough to freeze, then because container exec reachability failed during the online attempt. Each refusal was recorded rather than worked around. The last attempt succeeded and produced the one artifact the day actually delivered: a restorable, checksummed, digest-verified PostgreSQL archive that explicitly does not cover media, cluster-wide roles, or deployment coherence.

The other half of the day was quieter and structural. OpenAI Project materialization became an operator-invoked slice with a reserved thread-metadata key and an ADR, instead of an automatic import behavior. The Private Preview lane gained OpenAI chat in the Composer, longer account sessions, and Scout hosted project collection transport. A dependency-governed continuation campaign was defined, and its first work package was closed with an explicit refusal to claim a pass. An operator-facing design for model, price, and inference limits was drafted. And the release-truth document was refreshed on `main`, recording that the release gate is still HOLD and that no tracked qualification surface is current.

## Important outcome

The day's structural shift was in what the proof was willing to assert. A recovery procedure that starts by claiming a full-stack backup and ends by delivering a PostgreSQL-only logical dump is not a smaller success than the one it started with — it is the difference between a backup and a receipt. The artifact that exists now has a byte count, a SHA-256, a catalog listing, an isolated restore that exited zero, and digests that matched on Project ownership, thread membership, source grouping provenance, and message ownership links, and it names precisely what it excludes. That is a thing someone can verify later, rather than a thing someone was told.

The reason the claim could be narrowed honestly is that the serving identity was proven broken first. The bind-mounted checkout had advanced to `5b4ccfe33` while the running backend image stayed at `private-preview-tree-b46e2f42d`; the file digest confirmed the mismatch was real, and the receipt declined to guess which bytes the running process had loaded. Once that was established, the full-stack freeze could not be justified, so the PostgreSQL boundary was used instead — no service lifecycle operation, no schema write, no production volume operation, no materialization. The proof contracted to what it could actually stand behind.

The second shift is that materialization became an operator action with a reserved key rather than an automatic import behavior. The reserved-key design — four members, no canonical ID, ordinary writers preserving rather than overwriting, including malformed evidence, and permanent exclusion once applied — is what makes a replay safe. It also makes the precondition legible: ADR-112 requires a verified restorable backup before a first run, and marker absence alone cannot establish that the operation never happened. The day's checkpoint is what turned that precondition from a phrase into a checkable condition, and the DGC-01 receipt shows the same discipline applied where it could not yet be met: NEXT-PROOF-NEEDED, PASS prohibited, no provider invoked, no command invented.

None of this qualified a release surface. The gate is still HOLD, the operator inference work is still a draft, the OpenAI composer change is code on branches, and the sidebar mark is on a feature branch.
