# Inactive voice budget admission

Internal implementation candidate,2026-11-18. This is not an accepted activation
policy or enabled provider path. The existing
[budget foundation](../Plans/2026-11-14-inference-budget-foundation.md) and
ADR-003/005/087/091/092/110 remain governing; release truth remains HOLD.

## Authority boundary

Canonical User/thread/project/assistant-message state owns access. Existing fixed
operator-owned monthly/provider window rows own capacity. Internal quote/payer/
credential references are trusted service inputs, not newly verified grants.
There is no route, environment policy loader, issuer, seed, key lookup or provider
dispatch API. A claimed internal record does not grant permission to any adapter.
Production authorizers, spending quarantine, provider adapters, route posture and
clients are unchanged by this slice. No held Lens/continuous/Inspector/UI code or
the unmerged Lens migration is a prerequisite of this inactive primitive.

Each binding names one exact Agent connection attempt or standalone Speak chunk,
its account/thread/project and session or owned assistant message, normalized
content digest, credential-free settings/wire digest, opt-out, model/endpoint,
quote and policy identity. It retains no prompts, keys, headers, transcripts or
audio. Digests are metadata and do not prove the safety of an unknown preimage.
Use a fixed placeholder for rotating callback credentials before hashing settings.

Sponsor and account-owned routes are explicit immutable descriptors. Account-owned
owner must equal the invoking account; a different scope/credential/service fails.
There is no fallback to sponsor funds. Types do not prove credential ownership or
authorize reading a key. The future trusted resolver must establish each canonical
credential/payer/window mapping; coding-only execution custody is not chat/voice
BYOK authority. Deepgram speech and Guardian inference need separate service routes.

## Shared accounting and liability

Voice never manufactures an accepted ChatCompletionAttempt or renames time/
characters as tokens. Its separate tables share the actual aggregate monthly and
provider funding rows and deterministic locks with chat. Spent plus every held
liability counts before admission. There is no parallel money pool or per-process
counter granting capacity. Each operation binds exactly two original allocations.

Integer USD nanounit quotes have explicit typed units, provenance, validity,
rounding, minimum and fixed charges. Test quotes are synthetic; they do not prove
account eligibility, character-count semantics, cost-changing voice updates or
the provider's maximum billable connection/close interval.

Current canonical custody and exact descriptor are checked again at claim. A
committed claim permits at most one internal dispatch identity. Replay, changed
settings/content/payer, expired quote/window or insufficient held capacity refuses.
No network I/O occurs inside ledger transactions. A successful reserve is not a
provider permit, nor proof of a complete whole-session/whole-Speak integration.

Only never-claimed work may release its hold. Cancellation, timeout, missing audio,
crash or ambiguous commit cannot infer zero billing or mint another claim. Trusted
complete observations settle against the original quote; missing evidence stays
held, conflicting replay refuses and overruns are recorded honestly. Liability
survives account deletion and retired windows. Real reconciliation/retention actors
remain activation decisions; no automatic exceptional refund or erasure is added.

## Activation gates, not selected here

The production policy/credential resolver and exact final-provider claim consumer
remain absent. An enabled row, key, environment flag or fixture permit cannot fill
that boundary. Legacy quarantine remains inert without configured OpenAI bounded
mode; it is not a universal default-deny voice spending guarantee. No key should
be loaded merely because this inactive core is reviewable.

Before activation, root must accept issuer/revoker, exact payer/credential purpose,
eligible accounts/operations, verified funding windows/buffer, receipt/retention
authority and atomic daily allowance/reset policy. The proposed10 minutes/tester/
day is neither approved nor implemented. Every sponsored service, secondary call
and in-flight/unknown liability must share the sponsor aggregate. Actual Guardian
model costs and all standalone Speak chunks need independent exact admission.

Confirm project/account/model opt-out terms and tariff, billing minima/rounding,
start/stop/failed-start treatment and maximum close liability. Deepgram's
[Terms5.5](https://deepgram.com/terms) retain overage liability despite insufficient
credits and delayed balances. App allowances, credits or no payment card do not
establish Chris's no-unpaid-overage condition. No weaker canary exception, new
terms, paid add-on, account setting or provider probe is accepted here.

Continuous voice/Lens/client integration, native PostgreSQL/live rollback proof,
matched main deployment, callback/proxy/worker health and real opt-out/PCM/audio/
billing qualification are separate gated work. Preserve transient microphone
processing, no caller recording/training, selected voice families where supported,
no provider/mode substitution, no Think-Fast and generated-audio cache OFF.
