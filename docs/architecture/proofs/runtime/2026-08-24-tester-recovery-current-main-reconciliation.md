# Tester recovery and current-main reconciliation

## Result

**REVALIDATE LOCAL TRUTH — intervening local lineage changed the recovery seam:
`guardian/guardian_api.py`.**

The four-commit local evidence window added a bounded startup-failure receipt
wrapper at the application-lifespan boundary. Its stated intent is
observability and it re-raises the original exception, but it is a change to a
declared recovery-seam path. The local-window collision gate is therefore not
empty and this receipt does not authorize applying the Chroma recovery
candidate from the advanced local Tester branch.

The later healthy Tester observations do not supersede the previously matched
fresh-state Chroma condition: they reused Chroma state generated by a prior
failed first start, rather than exercising a genuinely absent Chroma path.
They establish a healthy subsequent startup and idle worker readiness only.

Current `main` preserves the candidate's source/test preimage and has no
equivalent recovery, but its current supported-Compose operator truth has a
separate earlier configuration-coherence blocker. A future task must reconcile
the lifespan-wrapper evidence and establish the current-main prerequisite
before applying or qualifying the candidate.
